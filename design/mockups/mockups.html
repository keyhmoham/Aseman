RULES
  • One filled colour block per screen at most (wallet, your bubble).
  • State = colour + word + shape. Never colour alone.
  • Saffron only for things waiting on the user.
  • No gradients, no blur, no glow. Depth comes from a 1px line and a sunken surface.
  • Numbers, times and keys use mono with tabular figures.
  • Built with Persian in mind: one family renders both scripts, layouts mirror for RTL.
